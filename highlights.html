HIGHLIGHT 01 • EDITORIAL & REVIVAL

Reviving a Cherished Dialogue: Sewa Se Samvad Tak, Samvad Se Badlav Tak

Reigniting the publication originally started by Late Ranjan Kumar Ji (1977–83 Batch), NOBA Samvad 2026 reconnects Hatians across batches, Ashrams, and continents with the timeless Netarhat values articulated by F. G. Pearce.

1
Pre-Editorial & Editorial (Pages 6–9): Memories of Upper & Lower Ghaghri, Paseripath, and Netarhat’s ethos transformed into an action call for present and past students.
2
Leadership & Co-Founder Perspectives (Pages 10–23): Reflections from Om Prakash Choudhary, N. N. Sinha, Rajesh Chandra Mishra, Co-Founders Jawahar Lal & Vikash Ranjan, and NOBA GSR India & Inc. teams.
HIGHLIGHT 02 • SANGINI FLAGSHIP MISSION

Project Sangini: Protecting Girls’ Health, Dignity & Education

Born from field visits to rural schools where menstrual hygiene barriers silently pushed girls out of classrooms, Sangini has scaled into a multi-state empowerment movement within four years.

1
725+ Schools Equipped: Installed Sangini Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) setups (sanitary pad vending machines & incinerators) across 10 Indian states including Bihar, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Gujarat, Telangana, Uttarakhand, and Maharashtra.
2
2.5 Lakh+ Adolescent Girls Reached: Dramatically reduced school absenteeism and built community-level awareness around menstrual dignity.
HIGHLIGHT 03 • SNEH & GRASSROOTS SEWA

SNEH (Support Network for Hatians) & Nationwide Relief Drives

Turning alumni bonds into both an organized mutual-support ecosystem and immediate disaster, heatwave, education, and ecological stewardship across India.

1
SNEH — Support Network for Hatians (Page 78): Structured mentorship, career guidance, and family crisis support linking Netarhat alumni across generations and geographies.
2
Disaster & Summer Relief (Pages 60–75): 25,000 sanitary pads, school bags & medicated mosquito nets during Bihar/Assam floods, plus 300+ large umbrellas distributed across 30+ NOBA chapters (with BJANA) in Delhi, Pune, Bengaluru, Lucknow, and Kolkata.
3
Education & Wildlife (Pages 70–71): Community Library at Late Padmashri Vashishth Narayan Center of Excellence (Kaimur, Bihar) and 4-year White Peacock enclosure adoption at Patna Zoo recognized by the Bihar Government.
