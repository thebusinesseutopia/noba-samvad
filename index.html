The NOBA GSR Philosophy

“Legacy Is Not What We Receive. It Is What We Build.”

Guided by F. G. Pearce’s timeless ideal — “the ‘come-on’ encouragement and example of a friend” — NOBA GSR brings together Netarhat alumni across generations, professions, and borders under one simple commitment: Any NOBA. Any Social Work. Anywhere.
